# Etsy Search Copywriting Guidance

Use this reference for every new listing and targeted copy tweak. It summarizes the official Etsy Seller Handbook pages supplied for this project and was reviewed on 2026-07-27. Etsy labels the underlying search guidance as current as of August 2025 and notes that search practices can change.

## Priority

Evidence integrity overrides optimization. Never add a keyword, attribute, occasion, recipient, material, size, style, performance claim, or other descriptor unless the local evidence supports it. Official Etsy guidance also stresses accurate, non-misleading presentation.

## Whole-listing approach

- Treat title, description, tags, category, and Etsy attributes as complementary search signals.
- Optimize for relevant query matching and buyer understanding, not a guaranteed rank.
- Prefer useful specificity over repeated keywords.
- Do not repeat an exact category or attribute phrase as a standalone tag when another supported phrase can cover distinct shopper intent.

## Category and attributes

- Choose the most specific defensible Etsy category.
- Suggest or mention only attributes that accurately describe what the product is.
- Skip attributes that are irrelevant or unsupported.
- Do not turn a possible use into a product attribute. For example, an ordinary item that could be gifted for Father's Day is not automatically a Father's Day product.
- Use verified color, material, pattern, size, room, occasion, recipient, or other category-specific facts as keyword inputs only when the evidence supports them.
- The Studio does not publish structured Etsy attributes yet. Preserve useful verified attribute facts in the evidence ledger and copy; do not invent placeholder values.

## Title

- State clearly what the item is, then add the strongest supported differentiators.
- Lead with the most useful buyer-facing phrase because the beginning is most visible while browsing, especially on mobile.
- Keep the title short, natural, and understandable at a glance.
- Do not create comma-separated or pipe-separated keyword lists.
- Keyword position is for readability, not a ranking trick.
- Keep the project limits: no more than 140 characters and normally fewer than 15 words.

## Description

- Naturally incorporate a few important relevant phrases in the first one or two sentences.
- Do not copy the title verbatim or open with a keyword list.
- Continue with supported buyer information in a scannable format.
- Keep the voice human and useful; omit facts that remain unknown or conflicted.

## Tags

- Aim to use all 13 tag opportunities only when 13 strong, distinct, evidence-supported phrases exist. Never add filler merely to reach 13.
- Keep every tag at 20 characters or fewer.
- Prefer descriptive multi-word phrases and supported long-tail intent over isolated generic words.
- Diversify tag meaning instead of repeating the same core phrase with minor word changes.
- Do not intentionally misspell terms, add needless singular/plural variants, or mix languages.
- Consider these supported angles when relevant: product type, distinctive feature, verified material or technique, style, size or shape, recipient, occasion, and solution.
- Recipient, occasion, gift, and solution phrases still require a defensible connection to the product. Do not manufacture intent from a generic item.
